# Sishir Electrical & Interior - Premium Web Strategy

## 1. Domain Name Ideas
For a high-end, premium brand offering both interior and electrical services, the domain should sound established, luxurious, and clear.

**Top Recommendations:**
1. **sishir.co** (Very premium, minimalist)
2. **sishirelectrical.com** (Clear, classic)
3. **sishirinteriors.com** (Focuses on the luxury aspect)
4. **sishirspaces.com** (Implies both interior environments and living spaces)
5. **studiosishir.com** (Design-focused, agency vibe)
6. **sishirdesign.in** (Great for Indian market targeting)

## 2. Tools & Stack Used
We've utilized a modern, high-performance stack suitable for a luxury brand:
*   **React & Vite**: Extremely fast page loads and smooth routing.
*   **Tailwind CSS**: For bespoke, pixel-perfect UI styling and responsive behaviors.
*   **Framer Motion**: To create the elegant, cinematic fade-ins, hover states, and parallax-like rendering without the heavy weight of full 3D libraries.
*   **Lucide React**: Minimal, crisp SVG icons.

*Note on 3D (Spline/Three.js)*: Because actual 3D rendering (like Spline) requires custom-made 3D models of rooms which take significant time to model in Blender or Spline, we simulated the 3D interaction using layered, high-quality images and CSS filtering. This achieves the "Lights On/Off" interactive experience incredibly well while keeping the website blazing fast. If Sishir Electrical has 3D renders from SketchUp/V-Ray, we can easily swap them into the immersive section!

## 3. UI Structure & Concept Delivery
*   **Hero Section**: Cinematic entry with a slow parallax zoom on a luxury interior.
*   **Services Module**: "Mastery in Design & Power" highlights the dual expertise, a key selling point.
*   **Interactive Showroom**: Simulated 3D light-switch environment to engage users directly.
*   **Story & Gallery**: Masonry layouts and brand story modules establish immense trust and quality.
*   **Contact / WhatsApp**: Immediate access to conversion-focused actions like WhatsApp scheduling.
